Relaybloom for iPhone and iPad

Privacy Policy

Relaybloom is an offline puzzle game from Firefly Lab. The iOS app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.

Game progress and settings

The game stores your puzzle progress, turns, badges, sparks and settings locally so you can resume play. The app does not transmit those records to Firefly Lab or third parties.

Retention and deletion

The game keeps its saved data while installed. To erase its local data, use Delete App in iOS Settings or from the Home Screen.

Support and privacy questions

Contact Firefly Lab for help with Relaybloom or questions about this policy. Please include your iOS version and a description of the issue when reporting a gameplay problem.
